## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-14
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Bloomberg reported this morning that soaring tanker-charter rates are cutting into refinery margins globally and beginning to constrain diesel supply availability — not just price. With IRGC attacks targeting 15+ vessels in October (including two supertankers struck Oct 11–12) and Brent holding near $105/bbl, tanker shortages are squeezing the economics of moving refined product to market. National diesel sits at $6.199/gal (EIA, Oct 5) — down from the $6.549 record — but the new attack activity threatens a reversal. For Uber Freight, this elevates the Hormuz story from a fuel-surcharge management challenge to a potential carrier-availability crisis: if diesel supply physically tightens in Q4, carriers cut operating days before rates rise — and the 4PL managed transportation book, where Uber Freight holds service commitments to shippers, has the least room to absorb that shock.

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Fuel Supply Risk, Not Just Fuel Price:** Bloomberg's Oct 14 report flags that tanker shortages could soon constrain diesel availability — not just price. If Q4 brings physical diesel scarcity, carriers reduce operating days before adjusting rates. Does Uber Freight's managed transportation service-level commitment architecture have a clear escalation protocol for fuel-scarcity scenarios — and have we communicated it to the clients most exposed on high-frequency, high-commitment lanes?

2. **Loadsmart Hit 50% SaaS GP — What's UF's Comparable Milestone?** The market will increasingly compare 3PLs on software revenue mix, not just freight volume. With Loadsmart crossing the threshold, the question is whether Uber Freight can isolate and demonstrate Transplace TMS platform revenue (vs. brokerage) as a distinct story for investors and enterprise shippers evaluating 4PL partners. What would it take on the product side to make that metric legible externally?

3. **Trimble TMS Displacement Window is Open Now:** Q4 RFP season is live, Trimble ownership is uncertain, and a three-way portfolio split is possible. This is a once-in-a-decade competitive window for Transplace. Do we have the pipeline coverage, migration tooling, and commercial motion to capture Trimble TruckMate and TMW customers at scale — before a new owner stabilizes the platform and Q4 procurement windows close?
