## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-03

Uber Freight's managed transportation customers need proactive scenario modeling now, before they come asking.

Differentiation via network depth and autonomous visibility tools is urgent. Confidence: High* (Sept. 3, 2026 — FreightWaves)
- **Parcel carriers (UPS, USPS, OnTrac):** All have now locked in Q4 peak-season surcharges following FedEx's July announcement — USPS pursuing a temporary 6% rate increase, UPS and OnTrac implementing demand surcharges. First synchronized peak surcharge stack across all major parcel carriers in years. *Implication for UF: Parcel cost inflation may push shippers toward truckload and LTL for bulkier freight in Q4, creating overflow demand for Uber Freight's brokerage during the peak surge. Confidence: High* (Sept. 3, 2026 — NTG Freight/Summar)

### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **FMCSA English Language Proficiency (ELP) NPRM:** FMCSA proposed codifying English-language proficiency as a formal out-of-service (OOS) criterion for CDL drivers — over 26,000 commercial drivers have already been sidelined under existing enforcement since the Trump administration reinstated strict ELP standards. Comment period closes October 9, 2026. If finalized, analysts estimate a 2–5% effective reduction in available driver supply, further tightening a market already running at +38% above year-ago spot rates. (Federal Register 2026-16288, Aug. 10; Sept. 3, 2026 — FreightWaves/FMCSA)

Does Uber Freight's Transplace 4PL platform have real-time scenario tools to help managed transportation customers model cross-modal cost impact across all three simultaneously — and are our account teams already in motion before customers come asking?
