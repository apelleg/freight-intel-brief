🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-14

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

A container ship was struck in the Strait of Hormuz yesterday (Sept 13), killing one person, as Gulf Arab diplomatic talks to ease the tanker war were postponed hours before they were set to begin. Hormuz throughput remains ~90% below pre-conflict levels, and national diesel hit $5.97/gal (EIA, week of Sept 7) — a new weekly nominal record eclipsing the June 2022 high. The collision of record fuel costs with the tightest U.S. truckload market since early 2022 (NTI $3.44/mi, +47% YoY) is Uber Freight's sharpest dual-margin threat entering Q4: small-carrier attrition accelerates as fuel squeezes operator margins precisely when peak-season volumes need that capacity, creating both spot volatility risk in brokerage and fuel-cost-sharing pressure in managed transportation.

🗂️ TMS & Managed Transportation

  1. Carrier network fuel exposure: At $5.97+/gal diesel, small carriers below operating-cost breakeven are parking equipment — compounding the capacity tightness entering Q4 peak. Aurora Investor Day prep (Sept 23): Aurora will reveal updated TaaS pricing, fleet scale trajectory, and capital needs in nine days — data that directly informs the competitive positioning of Uber Freight's Waabi partnership versus what shippers could access from Aurora direct.
