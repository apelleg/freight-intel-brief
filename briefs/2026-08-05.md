## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-05
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
ATA's July for-hire truck tonnage print — released this morning — rose just 0.3% month-over-month (index 113.7), the second near-flat reading in a row following June's −1.8% decline. The Cass Freight Index had already confirmed weak demand in June (shipments −4.1% YoY, expenditures +11.2% YoY). Yet DAT spot rates remain 40%+ above year-ago levels across every mode. The gap between flat demand and elevated rates is the market's loudest structural signal: capacity exits are doing the work that demand is not. For Uber Freight, this disconnect is the ideal sales environment for managed transportation — enterprise shippers are paying record-like rates without shipping more freight, and the case for outsourcing to a data-driven 4PL has never been stronger. The Q3 commercial window is now.
