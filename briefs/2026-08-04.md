## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-04
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
NHTSA published its Automated Driving System (ADS) Interim Framework Guidance in the Federal Register on July 31, 2026, opening a formal 31-day comment window through August 31. This is the first binding federal step toward a U.S. commercial driverless truck deployment framework — a concrete regulatory clock is now running, not just policy intent. For Uber Freight, the four-week deadline is time-sensitive: the brokerage already dispatches carrier partners using autonomous equipment, and a formal submission now is the most direct lever to influence the operational safety rules that will govern that dispatch relationship long-term.

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI
- **NHTSA ADS Interim Guidance:** Two parallel comment tracks are now open: 31-day window (closes Aug 31) for the Interim Framework operational rules, plus a year-long track through July 31, 2027 for long-form "Updating and Expanding Guidance on Safe Development of ADS." Industry players — Aurora, Waymo, and their carrier partners — can submit on both simultaneously. The compressed Aug 31 track governs near-term commercial operations; the longer track shapes deployment expansion standards. ([July 31, 2026 — Federal Register / NHTSA](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/))
- **Supply cycle & driver pool:** CDL enforcement targeting non-domiciled drivers is contracting the available driver pool fastest in produce and temperature-controlled corridors. Analysts project supply-driven capacity tightening through 2027 — structurally different from prior demand-led cycles — meaning this week's rate softening episode is unlikely to persist into sustained loosening. ([Aug 2026 — FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/))
