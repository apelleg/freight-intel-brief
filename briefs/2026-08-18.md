### 📋 Regulatory Watch

- **Canada Section 338 — T-0 TOMORROW (August 19):** 50% ad valorem duties take effect on $20B+ in Canadian goods including auto parts, dairy, lumber, and softwoods. No USMCA exemption applies. CBP has clarified it uses arrival/entry date — not ship date — as the determining event; freight already in transit that arrives after midnight tonight is subject to the full duty. FTZ entries after August 19 must elect privileged foreign status at time of admission. ([Aug 18, 2026 — GHY International](https://www.ghy.com/blog/canada-section-338-tariff-effective-date); [Aug 18, 2026 — Crane Worldwide Logistics](https://www.craneww.com/blog/section-338-ftz-guidance))

- **CVSA Brake Safety Week — August 23–29:** Annual roadside enforcement blitz focused on brake drums, rotors, and adjustment. Historical out-of-service rate ~14.3%. Sequential compression on top of Canada 338 disruption. ([Aug 2026 — CVSA](https://www.cvsa.org/programs-projects/brake-safety-week/))
