🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-25

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

Treasury Secretary Bessent's "Operation Economic Outcast" — announced August 24 — imposes secondary sanctions on 60+ entities worldwide facilitating Iranian trade in shipping, oil, crypto, gold, and aviation. This is the most aggressive US economic pressure on Iran since the Hormuz crisis began, and Iran immediately threatened to seize vessels violating its transit rules. Combined with Canada Section 338 tariffs (50% on $20B Canadian goods) now live since August 22, Uber Freight is navigating a simultaneous cross-border and fuel-cost shock: diesel at $5.45+/gal, North American supply chains repricing around two new trade disruptions at once, and the ATA's trade-policy advocacy apparatus suddenly headless after CEO Chris Spear's abrupt departure.
