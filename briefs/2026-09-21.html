🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-21

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

National diesel crossed $6.50/gallon for the first time today ($6.505, Bloomberg/AAA), gaining 87¢ in September alone and surpassing every prior nominal record including the 2022 Ukraine-war spike. For Uber Freight, this is a two-front threat: fuel surcharges embedded in legacy truckload contracts are now mathematically underwater for carriers, accelerating mid-cycle repricings and routing guide breakdown ahead of peak season; and J.B. Hunt's record intermodal quarter — alongside a Q3 earnings warning of 5–10% sequential decline from diesel shock — confirms that shippers are already switching modes. Transplace clients optimized for truck lanes in early 2026 need a modal review before Q4 locks them into the wrong cost structure.
