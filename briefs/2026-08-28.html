🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-28

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

Canada announced counter-tariffs of 15–50% on $27.6 billion in U.S. exports — 700+ product categories including steel, dairy, appliances, paper products, and electronics — effective September 8. This is Ottawa's first concrete retaliation to the U.S. Section 338 tariffs that went live August 22, and it hits the world's largest bilateral freight corridor: trucks carry more than half the value of US-Canada trade, with Detroit, Port Huron, and Buffalo absorbing the shock first. Cross-border freight managers have 10 days to reclassify, reroute, and advise customers — shippers with integrated supply chains face cumulative tariff stacking that significantly understates the nominal rates.

📋 Regulatory Watch

  • Canada Counter-Tariffs on U.S. Exports — Effective September 8: Finance Minister Champagne announced August 25 that Canada will impose tariffs of 15%, 25%, and 50% on 700+ U.S. product categories covering ~$27.6B in annual exports — steel, dairy, appliances, paper products, electronics, apparel, cosmetics, and tools. Goods in transit on Sept 8 are exempt. Trucks carry >50% of US-Canada freight value; Detroit, Port Huron, and Buffalo crossings absorb the primary impact. Managed transportation customers with US→Canada cross-border flows need immediate classification review. (Aug 25, 2026 — GHY Trade Compliance | Canada.ca)
  • UP-NS Merger: STB Formal Schedule Set, Final Decision Q2 2027: The Surface Transportation Board removed the Union Pacific–Norfolk Southern merger proceeding from abeyance August 18 and adopted a formal procedural schedule. Applicants' workpaper refiling deadline is today (Aug 28); final STB decision projected Q2 2027. If approved, the combined network would be the largest U.S. railroad, with significant implications for intermodal pricing on Midwest and Southeast corridors where Uber Freight competes with rail. (Aug 19, 2026 — STB / WOWT)
