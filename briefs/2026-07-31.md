## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-07-31
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Canada's Section 338 tariffs hit in **19 days (August 19)** — imposing 50% additional duties on ~$20 billion of Canadian goods with **no USMCA carve-out**. This is the first time in modern trade history Section 338 has been invoked, and unlike every prior tariff round, USMCA preferential treatment does not protect covered products (automotive, lumber, dairy, alcohol, machinery, consumer goods). For Uber Freight's managed transportation business, this is a planning deadline, not a future risk: Transplace-based 4PL clients with Canada-US lanes need lane-by-lane cost modeling, carrier reallocation, and routing guide updates completed *this week* — not in mid-August when border capacity will be contested. The 19-day window is the last clear runway for proactive client guidance before the disruption hits.

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI
- **AI infrastructure capex becomes explicit freight demand driver:** The Q3 2026 TD Cowen/AFS Freight Index identifies hyperscaler and AI data center construction capital expenditure as a **new primary freight demand driver** — pointing to a surprise +1.4% YoY jump in May trucking ton-miles coming from technology capex rather than consumer or industrial demand. TL rates now sit at a **4-year high** (17.7% above the baseline, +11% YoY); LTL rates hit an all-time record (76.8% above January 2018 baseline). The finding matters because traditional shipper segmentation models classify freight demand by consumer or manufacturing verticals and miss this new structural segment entirely. (July 31, 2026 — [Logistics Management](https://www.logisticsmgmt.com/article/td_cowen_afs_index_signals_persistent_price_pressure_across_truckload_parcel_and_ltl) / [CCJ Digital](https://www.ccjdigital.com/economic-trends/freight-demand/article/15829951/truckload-rates-hit-4year-high-amid-spiking-fuel-and-ai-tech-spend))

Hunt:** The agentic AI freight execution startup launched by JB Hunt and UP.Labs on July 21 is now in active discussions with additional carriers and enterprise logistics operators to deploy its platform beyond JB Hunt's internal network. The platform automates exception management, live data surfacing, and customer communications inside existing carrier workflows. Signal: the first carrier-native agentic AI layer is becoming a commercializable product — the line between carrier and tech vendor is blurring. (July 2026 — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/j-b-hunt-works-with-overroute-to-launch-freight-execution-platform))

(July 30, 2026 — [DAT](https://www.dat.com/blog/flatbed-report-industrial-demand-softens-pulling-rates-back-from-july-highs))

TMW shipper customers are entering Q4 TMS contract renewal cycles now — facing a decision on whether to re-sign with a platform whose ownership may change under a PE buyer, or migrate to an alternative. This is a time-sensitive competitive window for Transplace-based managed transportation: mid-market shippers in the TMW install base who have cross-border complexity or multimodal optimization needs are the highest-fit targets. (July 2026 — [Axios/StreetInsider](https://www.streetinsider.com/Hot+M+and+A/Trimble+Working+With+Goldman+To+Sell+Transportation+Unit+-+Axios/26740695.html))

- **Canada Section 338 creates Transplace activation moment.** With 50% tariffs hitting Canada-US lanes on August 19 and USMCA qualifying goods explicitly not exempt, managed transportation clients need their TMS platform to run cost impact models, reprice lanes, and reallocate to tariff-advantaged carriers or modes before the deadline. This is a concrete platform capability test — and an opportunity for Uber Freight to demonstrate Transplace's real-time lane optimization value to clients who might otherwise view TMS as a static routing engine.

### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **CBP/DOT joint enforcement crackdown on cross-border cabotage:** U.S. Customs and Border Protection is now sharing real-time data with DOT to actively identify Mexican drivers hauling freight between two domestic U.S. points — illegal cabotage. Reports indicate hundreds to potentially thousands of drivers have been blocked at the border in July. For Uber Freight's US-Mexico brokerage, this tightens USMCA corridor carrier capacity further at a moment when that corridor is already at a structural premium, and raises compliance risk for any network that sources carrier capacity without confirming driver cross-border authorization. (July 2026 — [FleetWorthy Compliance News](https://fleetworthy.com/resources/blog/compliance-news-july-2026/))
