## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-01
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Canada's dollar-for-dollar counter-tariffs on $27.6B of U.S. goods — steel, dairy, electronics, appliances — go live in **7 days (September 8)**. U.S. Trade Representative Greer confirmed no further trade talks are planned, locking in the escalation. For Uber Freight, this creates a two-wall tariff problem on US-Canada cross-border lanes: Canadian Section 338 duties (live since Aug 22) pressing costs on inbound Canadian freight, now matched by Canadian retaliatory duties squeezing U.S.-origin outbound moves. The Transplace 4PL business manages integrated supply chains across this corridor for enterprise shippers who urgently need landed-cost remodeling and mode optimization — the window to demonstrate that value is the next 7 days.
