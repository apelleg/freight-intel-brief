## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-13
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Iran escalated Hormuz attacks on October 11–12: a second supertanker caught fire after striking a naval mine while attempting to leave via an "unauthorized route," with at least 15 vessels targeted in October alone — and the IRGC explicitly warned its operations will "no longer be limited to the Strait of Hormuz." This lands at the worst possible moment for Uber Freight's Q4 planning: diesel had eased from a record $6.549/gal (Sept 28) to $6.199/gal (EIA week Oct 5) after Trump's Oct 9 no-strike signal, but renewed attacks threaten to reverse that relief — compressing carrier margins further just as tender rejections are already projected to reach 20% by December.

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Diesel FSC strategy for Q4 managed transportation:** Iran's Oct 11–12 supertanker mine attacks and the IRGC's warning that operations won't be confined to Hormuz could reverse the diesel pullback from $6.549 to $6.199/gal. Should Uber Freight proactively revise FSC methodology in Transplace contracts ahead of carriers doing it unilaterally — and can the product surface this fuel-risk signal to 4PL customers as a decision-support tool in real time?

2. **Panama Canal Oct 15 intermodal unlock:** Starting tomorrow, the Canal adds its 10th Neopanamax slot — the first structural ocean capacity increase since El Niño restrictions. With diesel still $6+/gal, the intermodal cost advantage on lanes >500 miles is at its widest in years. Does Uber Freight's Transplace platform today automatically surface intermodal alternatives for shippers with TL-only routing guides? If not, Q4 is live revenue being left on the table.
