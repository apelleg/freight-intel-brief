### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **CBP begins voiding Importer of Record numbers today (Sept. 18):** CBP will immediately void any IOR number linked to inaccurate or incomplete Form 5106 data under Executive Order 14411 ("Strengthening Customs Enforcement," June 3, 2026). A voided IOR blocks all imports under that number until CBP formally reestablishes it — affecting any shipper whose legal name, address, EIN, or contact information has changed since their last Form 5106 filing. *For Uber Freight: shipper customers whose IOR number is voided face immediate import shutdown — a direct trigger for managed transportation advisory and supply chain continuity engagement.* ([Sept. 18, 2026 — Clark Hill / FreightClear](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/cbp-form-5106-importer-number-enforcement-2026/))

1. **Carrier network breadth as a market-of-one advantage:** With at least one major competitor cutting its approved carrier list by 75%+, Uber Freight's ability to source from a larger vetted network is now a measurable differentiator in an acute capacity squeeze. Should the go-to-market team run targeted outreach to shippers currently running emergency minibids — positioning Uber Freight's carrier depth as the coverage solution competitors can no longer offer?

Does Uber Freight's brokerage and Transplace/4PL platform have automated fuel cost transparency and surcharge pass-through built in — or is this a product gap creating churn risk on both the carrier and shipper sides of the marketplace during a sustained volatile fuel cycle?

This creates acute, time-sensitive demand for a supply chain partner who can reroute freight, respond to CBP inquiries, and maintain operations continuity. Does the Transplace/4PL team have a proactive outreach playbook for shipper accounts at compliance risk — and is customs compliance advisory a whitespace capability in UF's managed transportation value proposition?
