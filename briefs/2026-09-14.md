## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-14
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
A container ship was struck in the Strait of Hormuz yesterday (Sept 13), killing one person, as Gulf Arab diplomatic talks to ease the tanker war were postponed hours before they were set to begin. Hormuz throughput remains ~90% below pre-conflict levels, and national diesel hit $5.97/gal (EIA, week of Sept 7) — a new weekly nominal record eclipsing the June 2022 high. The collision of record fuel costs with the tightest U.S. truckload market since early 2022 (NTI $3.44/mi, +47% YoY) is Uber Freight's sharpest dual-margin threat entering Q4: small-carrier attrition accelerates as fuel squeezes operator margins precisely when peak-season volumes need that capacity, creating both spot volatility risk in brokerage and fuel-cost-sharing pressure in managed transportation.

### 📊 Market Pulse (30 seconds)
- **Spot rates:** NTI $3.44/mi (+47% YoY); dry van linehaul $2.21/mi (+38% YoY), reefer $2.69/mi — spot has crossed above contract rates for the first time since early 2022. Tender rejection rate topped 15%, highest since 2022. (<a href="https://www.freightwaves.com/news/truckload-volumes-drop-15-but-spot-rates-stay-hot">Sept 8 week — FreightWaves/DAT</a>)
- **Load-to-truck:** Dry van 11.5:1, reefer 21.5:1, flatbed 36.4:1 — roughly double year-ago levels on flatbed. (<a href="https://www.freightwaves.com/news/truckload-volumes-drop-15-but-spot-rates-stay-hot">Sept 8 week — DAT</a>)
- **Notable capacity events:** Iranian commercial vessel struck near Hengam Island, Hormuz, Sept 13 (1 dead; crew evacuated); Gulf Arab maritime talks postponed. Diesel $5.97/gal nationally (EIA, Sept 7, 2026 — nominal weekly record). ATA August tonnage +0.2% MoM — first consecutive months of growth since early 2026, signaling demand-led volume is beginning to add to rate pressure, not just capacity attrition. (<a href="https://www.trucking.org/news-insights/ata-truck-tonnage-index-rose-02-august">ATA, Sept 2026 release</a>)

- **Aurora Innovation:** Presenting at Morgan Stanley 14th Annual Laguna Conference tomorrow (Sept 15, 11:30 am ET); full Analyst & Investor Day follows Sept 23 in Dallas — the first major capital markets event since Gen-2 truck launch across 10 Sun Belt driverless routes. *Implication for UF: Aurora is UF's commercial AV counterpart via Waabi; Sept 23 sets the public narrative on TaaS pricing (~$2/mi vs. $2.21/mi spot) and 200-truck year-end fleet target — positioning UF needs to track closely given its Waabi deployment pipeline.* Confidence: High (<a href="https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260902997149/en/Aurora-to-Host-Analyst-Investor-Day-on-September-23-2026">BusinessWire, Sept 2, 2026</a>)

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI
- **Aurora Driver 2 in commercial service:** Gen-2 driverless trucks — 1M-mile design life, ~50% lower hardware cost vs. Gen-1, built by Roush on a dedicated production line — are now operating on 10 U.S. Sun Belt routes. One customer committed to purchasing 500 Aurora Driver-equipped trucks; fleet target remains 200 driverless trucks by year-end 2026. All eyes on Sept 23 Investor Day for updated TaaS pricing and 2027 scale roadmap. (<a href="https://www.act-news.com/news/auroras-second-generation-driverless-trucks-enter-commercial-service-on-10-u-s-routes/">ACT News, July 2026</a>)
- **Morgan Stanley Laguna freight cluster (Sept 15):** Werner, J.B. Hunt, Heartland Express, Schneider National, and Aurora all present tomorrow. Expect public comments on Q4 capacity strategy, fuel surcharges, and contract repricing; this will anchor the market's pricing narrative entering peak season. (<a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/11/3360471/15800/en/heartland-express-inc-announces-participation-in-upcoming-conference.html">GlobeNewswire, Sept 11, 2026</a>)

### 🗂️ TMS & Managed Transportation
- **RedwoodConnect × Augment AI agent partnership** (Sept 10, 2026): Redwood Logistics' RedwoodConnect — the only iPaaS platform purpose-built for transportation — integrated Augment's AI agent "Augie" into its pre-built connector library. Shippers running Oracle Transportation Management, Blue Yonder, MercuryGate/Infios, or Manhattan Associates can now deploy AI-driven freight automation (exception handling, carrier assignment, scheduling) without custom development. This is a direct AI capability play in the same mid-market and enterprise TMS tier where Transplace competes, and it arrives at the start of Q4 RFP season. (<a href="https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/10/3359578/0/en/redwoodconnect-and-augment-partner-to-bring-faster-ai-integrations-to-more-tms-and-erp-providers.html">GlobeNewswire, Sept 10, 2026</a>)
- **Trimble TMS/TMW sale:** Goldman Sachs process ongoing; no buyer announced as of Sept 14. TMW and TM4 mid-market customers must now decide before Q4 whether to wait for M&A resolution or migrate — the highest-leverage Transplace displacement window of the year. (<a href="https://www.axios.com/pro/supply-chain-deals/2026/07/07/trimble-transportation-division-goldman-sachs-sale">Axios, July 7, 2026</a>)

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Carrier network fuel exposure:** At $5.97+/gal diesel, small carriers below operating-cost breakeven are parking equipment — compounding the capacity tightness entering Q4 peak. Do we have real-time visibility into which carriers in Uber Freight's network are margin-impaired, and have we pre-built surge routing guides or committed capacity programs for our highest-volume shipper accounts before October 15?

2. **TMS AI agent race:** RedwoodConnect/Augment just made AI freight automation available on Oracle TM, Blue Yonder, and MercuryGate without custom builds — competing platforms to Transplace. Does UF's 4PL/Transplace roadmap have a comparable AI agent integration in market before Q4 enterprise RFPs close, and if not, is a quick integration partnership faster than an internal build?

3. **Aurora Investor Day prep (Sept 23):** Aurora will reveal updated TaaS pricing, fleet scale trajectory, and capital needs in nine days — data that directly informs the competitive positioning of Uber Freight's Waabi partnership versus what shippers could access from Aurora direct. What commitments should UF leadership be prepared to discuss before the investor narrative publicly sets expectations?
