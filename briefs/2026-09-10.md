## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-10
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Canada's retaliatory tariffs on $27.6 billion in U.S. goods activated September 8 — placing 15–50% duties on steel, dairy, agricultural equipment, electronics, and plastics. This is the first concrete Canadian countermeasure since the USMCA negotiations collapsed and the U.S. imposed Section 338 tariffs on Canadian imports in August; both sides are now in full trade war. For Uber Freight, this directly disrupts the Canada-U.S. cross-border corridor: northbound freight for affected commodities faces new duty calculations, documentation requirements, and lane reconfiguration urgency — and 4PL clients expect their managed transportation partner to help navigate it in real time through the Transplace platform.

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI
- **DOT releases AV-5 National Strategy (September 3):** The Department of Transportation published its first comprehensive autonomous vehicle national strategy under the Self Drive Act. AV-5 establishes a federal deployment framework with standardized safety verification requirements and explicitly validates driverless commercial freight on interstate highway corridors. This is the policy signal the autonomous trucking sector has been waiting for — and directly accelerates Uber Freight's Waabi deployment pipeline. ([Sept 3, 2026 — DOT](https://www.transportation.gov/sites/dot.gov/files/2026-09/AV%20National%20Strategy_508_3Sep2026.pdf); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/09/the-road-ahead-for-driverless-vehicles-dot-unveils-av-5))
- **Gatik raises $200M Series D:** Middle-mile driverless truck specialist Gatik secured $200M (led by QIA and KDT), expanding its fixed-route autonomous delivery between distribution nodes. Gatik directly competes with hub-to-hub autonomous use cases Waabi and Aurora are targeting — and now has fresh capital to accelerate. ([Aug 25, 2026 — Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/edgarsten/2026/08/25/gatik-scores-new-200-million-investment-for-its-driverless-truck-tech/); [FleetOwner](https://www.fleetowner.com/technology/news/55401124/gatik-lands-200m-to-expand-driverless-middle-mile-truck-rollout))
- **TruckSmarter shuts down:** The owner-operator freight app announced it is ceasing operations. Even in an up-rate cycle with record owner-operator pricing power, the unit economics of building mobile-first carrier acquisition tools remain unworkable against incumbent load boards. ([Sept 2026 — FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucksmarter-shutting-down))

See Today's Top Signal for strategic context. Cross-border northbound freight for affected commodities requires immediate duty code classification and compliance routing. ([Sept 8, 2026 — Canada Finance Dept.](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/08/list-of-products-from-the-united-states-subject-to-counter-tariffs-effective-september-8-2026.html))

Non-compliant carriers face enforcement action; likely to accelerate exits among small fleets that couldn't fund upgrades, further tightening truckload capacity at the margin. ([Sept 8, 2026 — FMCSA](https://disa.com/news/2026-dot-compliance-updates-for-motor-carriers/))

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Transplace as a real-time tariff-compliance layer:** Canada's counter-tariffs on $27.6B in U.S. goods are live as of September 8, with potential for further escalation — no resolution in sight. What does Transplace currently offer 4PL clients on cross-border duty code alerting, routing alternatives for affected commodities, and carrier compliance documentation? If the answer is "not much," this is a product gap that is costing clients money today and creating churn risk.

2. **DOT's AV-5 strategy and Uber Freight's autonomous carrier pipeline:** The federal government just published its first comprehensive driverless truck framework, explicitly greenlighting commercial freight on interstate corridors. Uber Freight has skin in the game via the Waabi partnership and prior Aurora involvement. What is the product roadmap for onboarding autonomous operators as a carrier type in the Uber Freight platform — and do we have the carrier compliance, insurance, and tendering infrastructure ready when Waabi moves to commercial scale?

3. **Contract repricing in a spot-above-contract market:** Spot rates are 47% above year-ago and now exceed contract rates — the clearest signal yet that this cycle is structural, not seasonal. Are Uber Freight's contracted shippers being repriced aggressively enough, or is the brokerage absorbing the spread? Should the product team be building a real-time contract-performance vs. market-rate dashboard so commercial teams can walk into repricing conversations with data?
