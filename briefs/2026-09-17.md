**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Even as Iran formally unveiled a 60-day Hormuz shipping corridor to seven Gulf nations in Muscat (Sept 16), Iranian forces simultaneously attacked the bulk carrier Minoan Dignity and seized tanker Amara — a deliberate "corridor plus enforcement" strategy that keeps global energy markets hostage while appearing to negotiate. For Uber Freight, this convergence lands during Q4 contract finalization week: managed transportation customers need fuel hedge provisions today, and the brokerage desk faces a market where carriers are being squeezed from both sides.
