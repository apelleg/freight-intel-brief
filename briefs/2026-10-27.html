🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-27

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

Mexico's CANACAR launched an indefinite nationwide trucking stoppage Monday, October 26 — the same week US-Mexico bilateral USMCA talks stalled ahead of a soft November deadline. The strike is active across 19 states, with organizers estimating up to 21,000 daily border crossings worth ~$1.8B at risk. Stacked on top of the B-1 visa crackdown that already sidelined 25,000+ Mexican commercial drivers (ongoing since October), cross-border TL capacity is entering its tightest conditions of the 2026 cycle — exactly as Q4 peak demand builds. Uber Freight's cross-border brokerage and managed transportation customers with Mexico-origin freight face direct, acute exposure.
