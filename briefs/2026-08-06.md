### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI
- **FMCSA ADS NPRM — expected this month:** FMCSA's proposed rule for ADS-equipped commercial motor vehicles (RIN 2126-AC17) is projected for August 2026 — the first comprehensive federal operating framework for driverless commercial trucks. The rule would address driver qualification equivalencies, drug/alcohol testing, CDL requirements, pre-trip inspection protocols, and roadside inspection standards for ADS systems. This is the regulatory unlock that Aurora, Waabi, and plus.ai need to scale beyond individual route exemptions — and that Uber Freight's AV network product needs to expand commercially beyond the Texas Sun Belt. ([August 2026 — FMCSA](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/newsroom/safe-integration-automated-driving-systems-equipped-commercial-motor-vehicles); [FleetOwner](https://www.fleetowner.com/news/regulations/news/55390583/trucking-regulations-2026-inside-the-dot-fmcsa-agenda))

- **Pony.ai Robotruck scales in China:** At an August 3 media briefing, VP He Xing disclosed Q1 2026 Robotruck revenue of $10.2M (+31% YOY). Plans call for 500–1,000 Gen-4 heavy-duty trucks in China within 2-3 years across long-haul, bulk, and port logistics. A new L4 light-duty truck (co-developed with CATL) targets 100K units by 2030, with fully driverless operation projected to reduce per-km operating costs ~40%. China AV trucking is commercially real and scaling at a cost trajectory that will pressure US benchmarks within 2-3 years. ([August 6, 2026 — PR Newswire](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/08/06/tmt-newswire/pr-newswire/scaling-autonomous-freight-inside-ponyais-robotruck-business/2399790))

### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **Canada Section 338 tariffs — August 19 (13 days):** 50% additional duties on ~$20B in Canadian-origin goods (autos, dairy, lumber, machinery, electronics, cement, plywood) take effect at 12:01am EDT August 19. No USMCA carve-out — even USMCA-qualifying goods are subject. FTZ goods admitted on/after August 19 must enter under privileged foreign status. Cross-border freight operators should expect a demand surge on Canada-US lanes this week as importers rush final shipments. ([July 20, 2026 — White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-imposes-additional-tariffs-on-canada/); [August 2026 — Crane Worldwide Logistics](https://www.craneww.com/knowledge-center/trade-advisory-notices/trade-advisory-new-section-338-tariffs-add-50-duties-on-certain-canadian-imports-effective-august-19-2026/))
- **FMCSA ADS NPRM:** First comprehensive federal proposed rule for ADS-equipped trucks expected this month — see Freight Tech & AI above.

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **AI brokerage ROI as competitive currency:** RXO today showed AI-augmented operations producing $40M quarterly EBITDA and guided Q3 flat. Enterprise shippers and investors are now evaluating every broker by financial proof points, not just AI roadmaps. What's Uber Freight's equivalent public data point — load automation rate, carrier response time improvement, gross profit per load trend — and should we proactively develop a shipper-facing metric before Q3 earnings season?

2. **Canada Section 338 cross-border playbook — urgent:** In 13 days, $20B in Canadian-origin goods face 50% additional tariffs with no USMCA exemption. Our Transplace managed transportation customers with Canadian suppliers need active lane replanning now. Can the 4PL advisory team deploy a proactive playbook this week — carrier network shifts, FTZ analysis, alternate routing — that turns tariff chaos into a managed transportation retention and expansion win?

3. **FMCSA ADS NPRM — product architecture question:** The first federal rule for driverless trucks is expected this month and will define who holds the safety obligations a driver used to hold. For Uber Freight's AV network product, this shapes whether UF should position as the carrier-of-record alternative, a pure marketplace, or a network operator. Have we mapped the likely regulatory obligations against UF's AV product architecture before the NPRM drops?
