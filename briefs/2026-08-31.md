## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-31
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
NHTSA published two Federal Register extensions today (2026-17741 and 2026-17742) pushing both the AV Framework interim guidance comment period and the FMVSS Exemption IFR deadline from August 31 to September 30, 2026, granted at the request of the Alliance for Automotive Innovation. This directly delays the regulatory runway for Aurora's commercial deployment exemptions — the same framework governing Uber Freight's named AV carrier partnership — and functionally holds FMCSA's ADS NPRM (RIN 2126-AC17) for another month. With Aurora presenting at Goldman Sachs on September 10 and Gatik closing a $200M Series D last week, the AV freight sector is accelerating commercially just as its federal framework stalls.

### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **NHTSA ADS Framework + FMVSS Exemption IFR — 30-day extension:** NHTSA published Federal Register notices 2026-17741 and 2026-17742 today extending both the AV Framework interim guidance comment period and the FMVSS Exemption IFR from August 31 to September 30, 2026. Extension was requested by the Alliance for Automotive Innovation. The FMVSS Exemption IFR governs the ADS-operated commercial vehicle exemptions Aurora requires for driverless revenue operations; FMCSA's ADS NPRM (RIN 2126-AC17) is functionally sequenced after this IFR, pushing that rulemaking to September/October at earliest. Uber Freight, as Aurora's named commercial network partner, has standing to file comments through September 30 ([Aug 31, 2026 — Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/31/2026-17741/))
- **Canada Section 338 counter-tariffs — T-8 days:** Canada's retaliatory tariffs on ~$60B of US goods take effect September 8. Goods already in transit are exempt. CHR issued a shipper advisory on August 28 recommending cross-border load acceleration this week for affected commodities (steel, dairy, appliances, electronics). Enterprise Transplace 4PL customers with US–Canada lanes in those categories should have advisories in-hand ([Aug 28, 2026 — C.H. Robinson Advisory](https://www.chrobinson.com/en-gb/resources/insights-and-advisories/client-advisories/2026q3/08-28-26-client-advisory-update-government-canada-response-united-states-section-338-tariffs/))
- **STB Union Pacific / Norfolk Southern merger review restart:** STB formally restarted the UP/NS merger review (PR-26-21) with a November 18 comment deadline. If approved, would create a ~$150B railroad combining the two largest western and eastern Class I carriers, with significant implications for intermodal competitive dynamics and shipper modal shift ([Aug 2026 — STB / Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/stb-restarts-union-pacific-norfolk-southern-merger-review-and-sets-november-18-comment-deadline/))

1. **AV Comment Window — Does UF Have a Filed Position?** NHTSA's September 30 deadline is now a live window for Uber Freight to shape how the FMVSS Exemption IFR treats brokers in ADS-operated commercial vehicle dispatch. Has product/policy aligned on whether UF files comments, and what position on broker role in driverless dispatch should UF advocate for?

What is UF's middle-mile AV strategy, and should the Transplace 4PL platform be positioned to coordinate autonomous middle-mile as a managed service rather than cede it entirely to closed carrier–shipper loops?
