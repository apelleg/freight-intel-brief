### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Aurora DaaS / UF Commercial Role:** Hirschbach just committed to 500 Aurora DaaS trucks at $0.85/mi starting 2027, and UF is Aurora's named commercial partner. Does UF's current partnership agreement position it as a DaaS fleet operator, a capacity broker of Aurora TaaS miles, or a 4PL integrating autonomous capacity into managed transportation contracts? The answer carries very different margin profiles and should shape product and commercial investment decisions for the next 12 months.

2. **Hormuz Binary Outcome / Rate Lock Strategy:** Today's UNGA talks create a binary Q4 and 2027 scenario: if Iran reopens Hormuz within 7 days, diesel could fall $1–2/gal and spot rates would reprice materially lower; if talks collapse, CHR's +10% dry van 2027 forecast becomes the floor. How should UF advise its 4PL clients right now on locking vs. floating for 2027 contracts — and what is UF's internal hedge on its own brokerage book against either outcome?

3. **Trimble Displacement Playbook:** The Q4 2026 RFP window is live and Trimble's platform uncertainty is at its peak with no buyer named. Does Transplace have the enterprise sales motion, onboarding capacity, and product completeness to run a proactive outbound campaign against mid-market TMW/TM4 accounts in October–November — or does the constraint sit on the implementation and integration side rather than the sales side?
