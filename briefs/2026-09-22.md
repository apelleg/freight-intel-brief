## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-22
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Uber Freight announced a major European 4PL expansion today: Mike Doucleff named Head of Europe (reporting directly to CEO Rebecca Tinucci), a new Control Tower and operations hub launching in Krakow, Poland in 2027, and dedicated product and engineering resources committed to improving the European TMS experience. Coming one week after the OXEA global managed transportation mandate, this is Uber Freight's clearest statement yet that 4PL is a global, not merely North American, growth vector — and that European TMS investment is now explicitly on the product roadmap. For a CPO one hundred days into the role, this defines the geographic scope of the platform buildout: European customs workflows, shipper interfaces, and lane economics all need representation on the near-term roadmap. (Sept 22 — [GlobeNewsWire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3365961/0/en/uber-freight-expands-european-business-with-new-leadership-and-investment.html))

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **European TMS scope — what we just committed to:** Today's announcement explicitly included "dedicated product and engineering resources focused on improving the TMS experience for European shippers." Before Krakow hiring begins, define what that scope actually is: European feature parity on Transplace, a new shipper-facing orchestration layer, or targeted custom workflows for VAT/customs compliance? An undefined commitment in a capital-constrained environment will compound fast.

2. **Aurora Investor Day (tomorrow, 8:45 AM CT, ir.aurora.tech):** Aurora resets its 2028 revenue and fleet targets tomorrow — these numbers define what the UF/Waabi autonomous capacity product can realistically look like for shipper routing guides in 2027. What fleet size and route coverage milestone would let UF offer driverless capacity as a committed lane option, and does tomorrow's reset get us there?

3. **Routing guide failures as a 4PL sales trigger:** JB Hunt confirmed today that shipper routing guides are crumbling at an accelerated pace — precisely the condition that makes 4PL managed transportation compelling. Does UF's brokerage team have a systematic trigger to identify customers with elevated tender rejection rates and route them to a proactive 4PL conversation before they run a competitive RFP?
