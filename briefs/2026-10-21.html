⚡ Today's Top Signal

Iran Foreign Minister Araghchi stated explicitly on October 21 that the Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until the United States agrees to Iran's full terms — extinguishing hopes for a near-term diplomatic resolution and pushing Brent crude to $109.73–$110.46/bbl. Goldman Sachs estimates total global crude freight rates now exceed $20/barrel (vs. $3 a year ago), with Hormuz war-risk premiums alone accounting for $7/barrel of that increase. For Uber Freight, the consequences split by business line: the brokerage benefits from supply-driven spot rate strength (+35% YoY van rates), but 4PL managed transportation clients face simultaneous diesel cost spikes ($6.28/gal nationally), accelerating carrier financial attrition, and Q4 demand peaks — with no near-term relief on the horizon.
