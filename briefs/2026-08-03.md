### 📋 Regulatory Watch

- **Canada Section 338 tariffs — T-16 days (effective August 19):** 50% additional duties on Canadian imports take effect August 19 with no USMCA carve-out. Exclusions are narrow: energy products, and goods already subject to Section 232 (steel, aluminum, autos/parts). Covered goods include wood, paper, dairy, alcohol, machinery, furniture, and textiles — affecting an estimated $20B in annual Canadian imports. Any managed transportation client with unhedged Canadian lanes needs repricing and carrier allocation decisions this week. (USTR / Crane Worldwide Logistics)

- **Mexico MVE effective August 1:** Electronic customs value declarations now mandatory for Mexico imports — see TMS & Managed Transportation for Transplace implications. (August 1, 2026 — Mexico SAT)
