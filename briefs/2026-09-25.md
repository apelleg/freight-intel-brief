## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-25
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Super Typhoon Ragasa made landfall on the South China coast on September 24, disrupting Shanghai and Ningbo port operations with 661 vessels already impacted or at risk, pushing Shanghai–LA transpacific spot rates to $7,185/FEU. The compounding factor for domestic freight: JB Hunt this week announced its intermodal capacity has hit its ceiling, forcing overflow freight onto spot truck — at a moment when tender rejections are back above 14% and diesel sits at a record $6.53/gallon. For Uber Freight, this is a brokerage revenue moment: dray and spot truck demand on the West Coast is about to spike, but 4PL clients simultaneously face mounting fuel surcharges and expedite costs that Uber Freight's managed transportation team will need to absorb or renegotiate.

- **Notable capacity events:** Super Typhoon Ragasa disrupts Shanghai/Ningbo (Sept 24 — [Freightos](https://www.freightos.com/freight-resources/typhoon-disruptions-driving-congestion-and-putting-pressure-on-rates-september-25-2026-update/)); LA/LB container dwell averaging 6.5 days and worsening; JB Hunt intermodal at capacity ceiling, overflow to spot truck; US September container imports down 8.4% amid tariff uncertainty ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/us-september-container-imports-dip-153027622.html))

### 🏢 Competitor Moves
- **C.H. Robinson:** Repricing contracts and raising shipper rates as capacity stays tight; September freight update forecasts dry van contract costs up ~10% YoY into 2027. Nuclear verdict appeal ($604M) remains in legal limbo — the Dallas County judge has still not affirmed the July 23 ruling, delaying the formal appeal clock. *Implication for UF: CHR's aggressive repricing creates a contract renewal tailwind for Uber Freight heading into 2027 bid season; the verdict delay extends CHR's balance-sheet uncertainty and potential distraction into at least 2027.* Confidence: High (Sept 25, 2026 — [Supply Chain Dive](https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/ch-robinson-rates-contract-spot-capacity-xpo-brokerage-truck/599589/), [CCJ](https://www.ccjdigital.com/regulations/safety-compliance/article/15830826/ch-robinson-to-appeal-604-million-freight-broker-liability-verdict))
- **Kodiak AI / DTL Transport:** Completed first autonomous produce deliveries in California under a new CA DMV heavy-duty AV Testing permit — 225-mile Fresno→LA runs on SR-99 and I-5 with a safety driver present; plans driverless Texas operations by year-end. With Roush Industries targeting 1,000-unit annual production of Kodiak Driver-equipped trucks, Kodiak is now the second AV player with permitted freight routes in two major states (Texas + California). *Implication for UF: Uber Freight's Waabi partnership now faces a competitor directly in the I-5/SR-99 reefer corridor — California's highest-volume produce lane.* Confidence: High (Sept 24, 2026 — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/24/3368370/0/en/kodiak-ai-and-dtl-transport-complete-first-autonomous-trucking-deliveries-under-new-california-dmv-permit.html), [Heavy Duty Trucking](https://www.truckinginfo.com/news/kodiak-hauls-produce-in-california-autonomous-truck-pilot))
- **Small carriers — 16 bankruptcies in < 1 month:** FreightWaves counted 16 Chapter 11/7 filings through Sept 21 spanning carriers from 40 to 300 tractors (including Xoco Transport, 40 tractors, filed Sept 16). *Implication for UF: Accelerating carrier exit keeps supply tight and spot rates elevated; but it also shrinks the approved-carrier pool, reinforcing Uber Freight's carrier vetting infrastructure as a moat against brokers with weaker compliance capabilities.* Confidence: High (Sept 21, 2026 — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/16-trucking-companies-hit-bankruptcy-court-in-less-than-a-month))

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI
- **AV competitive map: Kodiak in California** (Sept 24 — see Competitor Moves). With Aurora tracking toward 200 driverless trucks by year-end and Volvo launching 300+ Aurora-powered VNLs in Q1 2027, the deployment race has shifted from "who's first?" to "who's scaling fastest in which corridors?" The California reefer lane now has a permitted AV pilot competitor. ([CNBC Aurora](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/aurora-autonomous-semi-ride.html), [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/24/3368370/0/en/kodiak-ai-and-dtl-transport-complete-first-autonomous-trucking-deliveries-under-new-california-dmv-permit.html))
- **AI commoditizing in trucking operations:** Heavy Duty Trucking and TruckersReport both published Sept 25 features confirming AI is now "core" to fleet dispatch, predictive maintenance, and brokerage — no longer experimental. The differentiation window is narrowing: the moat is shifting from "having AI" to owning proprietary data at scale and the logistics graph behind it. (Sept 25, 2026 — [Heavy Duty Trucking](https://www.truckinginfo.com/articles/ai-is-reshaping-trucking-in-2026-from-the-back-office-to-the-shop))

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Typhoon → JB Hunt ceiling → spot surge — is our brokerage desk positioned?** Typhoon Ragasa will amplify West Coast dray and spot demand this week just as JB Hunt intermodal overflows to truck. Does Uber Freight's brokerage have activated playbooks for this kind of sudden capacity-demand dislocation, and are our 4PL account managers proactively reaching out to enterprise clients with ocean-to-truck splits before they call us in crisis mode?

2. **Q4 TMS RFP window: are we capitalizing on the Trimble uncertainty?** Trimble/TMW customers are actively evaluating alternatives during exactly the window when Q4 enterprise technology RFPs are in flight — and project44 is the most visible challenger with 57% YoY growth and fresh IDC validation. What is our managed transportation Q4 pipeline, and how aggressively is the sales team positioning Transplace as the destination for TMW accounts looking to move?

3. **Waabi vs. Kodiak vs. Aurora — what's our 90-day deployment milestone?** Kodiak is now permitted in California (Uber Freight's reefer corridor) and Aurora is on track for 200 driverless trucks by year-end. The Waabi partnership was an industry first, but the competitive landscape has compressed. What is the next concrete deployment milestone for the Waabi/Uber Freight integration, and does Amir need to convene an AV roadmap review before Q4 planning closes?
