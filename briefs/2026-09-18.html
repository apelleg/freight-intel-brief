📋 Regulatory Watch

  • CBP begins voiding Importer of Record numbers today (Sept. 18): CBP will immediately void any IOR number linked to inaccurate or incomplete Form 5106 data under Executive Order 14411 ("Strengthening Customs Enforcement," June 3, 2026). A voided IOR blocks all imports under that number until CBP formally reestablishes it — affecting any shipper whose legal name, address, EIN, or contact information has changed since their last Form 5106 filing. For Uber Freight: shipper customers whose IOR number is voided face immediate import shutdown — a direct trigger for managed transportation advisory and supply chain continuity engagement. (Sept. 18, 2026 — Clark Hill / FreightClear)
