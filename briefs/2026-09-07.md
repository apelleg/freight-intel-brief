## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-07
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Canada's counter-tariffs on U.S. goods go live at 12:01 a.m. ET tomorrow, September 8 — 15–50% duties on $27.6B in U.S. exports covering steel, dairy, appliances, electronics, and agricultural equipment. Layered on top of the U.S. Section 338 tariffs already hitting $20B in Canadian imports since August 22, cross-border trade now has a full two-wall tariff structure: northbound U.S. goods face Canada's retaliation; southbound Canadian goods face U.S. 50% duties. Carriers are already pulling back from northbound loads, threatening volume imbalances and equipment displacement on one of the most capacity-dense North American corridors. For Uber Freight's brokerage and Transplace-based 4PL managed transportation business, this demands same-day cross-border network reoptimization — re-tendering, HTS reclassification, and alternative-lane activation — for any shipper with northbound Canadian exposure starting tomorrow morning.
