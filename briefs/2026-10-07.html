🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-07

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

President Trump signed an executive order today permitting highway use of tax-free red-dyed (off-road) diesel through December 31, deferring the 24.4-cent/gallon federal excise tax with no interest or penalties — the first direct diesel cost relief for truckers since the Hormuz supply shock began. The move saves carriers roughly $61 per 250-gallon fill-up federally (up to $100+ if states follow suit), providing immediate cash-flow relief to small fleets that have been filing for bankruptcy at a record pace. For Uber Freight, the key watch: most FSC agreements are tied to the DOE benchmark diesel price, which tracks standard highway diesel — if carriers begin blending red-dye purchases, actual pump prices may diverge from the benchmark, creating a mismatch in fuel surcharge calculations across UF's contract portfolio.
