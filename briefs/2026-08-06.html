🔧 Freight Tech & AI

  • FMCSA ADS NPRM — expected this month: FMCSA's proposed rule for ADS-equipped commercial motor vehicles (RIN 2126-AC17) is projected for August 2026 — the first comprehensive federal operating framework for driverless commercial trucks. The rule would address driver qualification equivalencies, drug/alcohol testing, CDL requirements, pre-trip inspection protocols, and roadside inspection standards for ADS systems. This is the regulatory unlock that Aurora, Waabi, and plus.ai need to scale beyond individual route exemptions — and that Uber Freight's AV network product needs to expand commercially beyond the Texas Sun Belt. (August 2026 — FMCSA; FleetOwner)
  • Pony.ai Robotruck scales in China: At an August 3 media briefing, VP He Xing disclosed Q1 2026 Robotruck revenue of $10.2M (+31% YOY). Plans call for 500–1,000 Gen-4 heavy-duty trucks in China within 2-3 years across long-haul, bulk, and port logistics. A new L4 light-duty truck (co-developed with CATL) targets 100K units by 2030, with fully driverless operation projected to reduce per-km operating costs ~40%. China AV trucking is commercially real and scaling at a cost trajectory that will pressure US benchmarks within 2-3 years. (August 6, 2026 — PR Newswire)
