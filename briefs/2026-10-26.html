⚡ Today's Top Signal

Commerce Secretary Lutnick remains the primary blocker, refusing to move on automotive tariff cuts, while 25,000+ Mexican commercial drivers remain sidelined by CBP visa revocations. For Uber Freight, which flagged cross-border TL as its highest Q4 exposure sector in the September market report, a hard miss on the October 29 date with no framework agreed could trigger a new wave of routing-guide disruptions and accelerate spot-rate pressure on Mexico-U.S. loads heading into peak season.
