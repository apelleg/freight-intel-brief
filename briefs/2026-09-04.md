## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-04
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

---

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Four shocks are converging on the freight market simultaneously today: (1) The Panama Canal's new 34-vessel/day transit cap begins this morning — a hard infrastructure reduction with a further cut to 32/day on September 15; (2) all Dutch ports (Rotterdam, Amsterdam, Zeeland) are on an 8-hour strike today, overlapping with concurrent German port disruptions; (3) Canada's retaliatory counter-tariffs are four days from going live, with the Canadian Trucking Alliance warning of a "nuclear winter" for Canada-US cross-border freight and USTR Greer confirming no further bilateral trade talks are planned; and (4) the freight market enters Labor Day weekend at a 15-week spot rate low with a seasonal reversal expected this week. For Uber Freight, the cross-border brokerage book and 4PL managed transportation clients with Canada-sourced supply chains are in the direct path of the most acute disruption.

Rates at 15-week seasonal low heading into Labor Day — a post-holiday reversal is widely expected. Diesel $5.599/gal as of Aug 31 (+$1.865 YoY). DAT's August monthly report shows van TVI at the softest August since 2021, but a month-end spike from 9.62→12.01 L/T in a single week signals underlying capacity attrition. ([Sept 2, 2026 — DAT](https://www.dat.com/blog/dry-van-report-spot-rates-ease-as-summer-freight-demand-softens))

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI

- **TruckSmarter shuts down today (Sept 4):** The AI-native load board and chat-based dispatch app for owner-operators closes permanently following an undisclosed acquisition. Over 500,000 carriers used the platform, which featured Dispatch — an AI agent that let drivers book freight in plain language rather than working a traditional load board. The company raised $16M in September 2025 for growth, making the abrupt exit particularly notable. The acquiring company has not been named. ([Sept 2-4, 2026 — FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucksmarter-shutting-down); [Source Alliance](https://sourcealliance.net/trucksmarter-shutting-down/))

- **Aurora Innovation — Analyst & Investor Day (Sept 23):** Aurora confirmed an Analyst & Investor Day at its Pittsburgh headquarters on September 23 — the first major institutional showcase since the company disclosed its $2+/mi TaaS and $0.85+/mi DaaS pricing. The Goldman Sachs Communacopia conference on September 10 will be the first public forum for pricing scrutiny, directly relevant to Uber Freight's commercial partnership with Aurora. ### 📋 Regulatory Watch

- **FMCSA English Language Proficiency NPRM (Federal Register 2026-16288):** FMCSA proposes codifying English language proficiency as an out-of-service (OOS) criterion for commercial drivers. An estimated 26,000+ drivers are already sidelined; the formal comment period closes October 9. If finalized, the rule would most acutely tighten carrier capacity in US-Mexico cross-border lanes — a corridor Uber Freight operates and that is already under pressure from ongoing cabotage enforcement actions. ([Sept 3, 2026 — FMCSA](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov))
