## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-12
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

The AV freight market is now operational across four simultaneous commercial deployments: Waabi/Warp completed its first commercial week on Dallas-Houston (launched Oct 5), Kodiak AI launched a new 435-mile Dallas-Laredo lane with Charger USA (Oct 8), Einride began daily driverless electric freight in Ohio (Oct 6), and Aurora remains on track for 200 driverless trucks by year-end. For Uber Freight, the Waabi TaaS partnership is now generating real freight revenue — validating the commercial AV brokerage thesis — but Kodiak simultaneously opened the Dallas-Laredo corridor that sits at the heart of UF's US-Mexico cross-border volume. The window to define which corridors the Waabi partnership owns, before competitors lock them in, is open right now.

### 📊 Market Pulse (30 seconds)
- **Spot rates:** DAT van all-in ~$3.08/mi nationally (Oct 2026 — [DAT Trendlines](https://www.dat.com/trendlines)); linehaul $2.25/mi for week Sept 27–Oct 3 (+43% YoY, +7¢ WoW); SONAR contract TL $2.72/mi+fuel (+18% YoY, 52-week high) (Oct 9, 2026 — FreightWaves/SONAR)
- **Load-to-truck:** Van L/T 13.7 (week Sept 27–Oct 3, quarter-end surge; prior weeks ~11.1–11.2); reefer 19.1; tender rejections ~14% (~3× 2023 baseline)
- **Notable capacity event:** Crude oil flows through Hormuz recovered to pre-war baseline via pipeline rerouting — but refined product exports remain at only 677K bpd (19% of the 3.6M bpd pre-war level), structurally keeping diesel elevated (Oct 11, 2026 — [Forbes/Kpler](https://www.forbes.com/sites/kensilverstein/2026/10/11/trump-blames-kyiv-for-diesel-but-the-barrels-went-missing-in-hormuz/)). EIA diesel $6.199/gal week of Oct 5 (down from $6.549 Sept 28 record); AAA spot crept back to $6.28/gal by Oct 8. Domestic intermodal at all-time volume high (+8% YoY) as diesel economics drive modal shift on lanes >500 miles (Oct 9, 2026 — FreightWaves).

### 🏢 Competitor Moves

- **Waabi / Warp + Uber Freight**: Completed first commercial week on the Dallas-Houston LTL corridor (launched Oct 5, 2026 — Waabi/FreightWaves). Uber Freight is the carrier network partner and commercial route developer for Warp. *Implication for UF: Week-one operational data (load counts, on-time performance, customer acceptance) is now available internally — this is the foundation of the Q4 and 2027 scale-up business case.* Confidence: High

- **Kodiak AI + Charger USA**: Launched 435-mile Dallas-Laredo autonomous freight lane (Oct 8, 2026 — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/?p=582819)). Safety driver still in cab; Kodiak safety case at 96% completion; driverless launch targeted later in 2026; first AV freight lane serving Laredo, the #1 US-Mexico land port by volume. *Implication for UF: Kodiak now operates on two commercial AV corridors (Dallas-Houston via IKEA, Dallas-Laredo via Charger) that directly overlap UF's highest-volume US-Mexico and Texas lanes — the brokerage race for AV corridor lock-in is underway.* Confidence: High

- **Einride + EASE Logistics**: Launched daily driverless Level-4 electric freight between warehouses in Marysville, Ohio (Oct 6-7, 2026 — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/?p=582530)/CCJ Digital). Cabless trucks, remote operator monitoring, no safety driver onboard; part of $8.8M ODOT/DriveOhio initiative. *Implication for UF: AV freight is no longer confined to Texas sun-belt corridors — the Midwest industrial belt is in play, expanding the geographic TaaS addressable market and raising shipper expectations for AV availability.* Confidence: High

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI

- **Freight MCP ecosystem achieves critical mass**: Two major freight intermediaries — Flexport (Oct 6) and Freight Right Global Logistics (Oct 9) — launched MCP APIs within 5 days of each other, enabling AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot) to book and track ocean and cross-border freight programmatically at negotiated rates, without human login (Oct 9, 2026 — [FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/?p=570951)). The MCP pattern is standardizing AI-to-freight-execution integration faster than expected; UF's own API/integration surface for managed transportation customers will be evaluated against this standard in 2027 RFPs.

- **2027 3PL Study confirms shippers are outpacing their 3PLs on AI adoption**: Shippers report 66% AI adoption (predictive analytics, risk planning) vs. only 43% for 3PLs — widening the expectation gap (Oct 7-8, 2026 — [SDCExec](https://sdcexec.com/transportation/3pl-4pl)/[TheTrucker](https://thetrucker.com/trucking-news/business/2027-third-party-logistics-study-puts-ai-partnerships-and-supply-chain-resilience-in-the-spotlight)). Top 3PL barriers: data quality and availability (61%), legacy system integration (54%), budget (46%). For UF's Transplace-based managed transportation business, this gap is the core product/engineering challenge for 2027.

### 🗂️ TMS & Managed Transportation

- **Trimble TMS sale still in limbo through Q4 RFP season**: CEO confirmed multiple external parties remain interested in the Transportation division (~Oct 1, 2026 — [AMB Logistic](https://amblogistic.us/trimble-keeps-freight-tech-options-open-while-accelerating-its-ai-strategy/)). Apax and Bain have stepped back; a third buyer is evaluating whether to pursue Transporeon (European) and TMW/TM4 (North American) separately. With Trimble ownership unresolved and Q4 enterprise TMS RFPs now live, enterprise shippers are evaluating alternatives with no Trimble roadmap certainty — the clearest Transplace displacement window in years.

### 📋 Regulatory Watch

- **FMCSA ELP NPRM comment period closed Oct 9**: Over 1,800 comments received on docket FMCSA-2026-0826; the proposed rule would codify English language proficiency failures as out-of-service violations, potentially sidelining ~9,000 additional cross-border drivers on top of 26,000+ already out of market (Oct 9, 2026 — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/10/2026-16288/english-language-proficiency-out-of-service-criteria)/FreightWaves). FMCSA is now reviewing comments with no published timeline for a final rule. Combined with diesel economics, this represents the second cross-border supply-side constraint at active regulatory risk entering Q4.

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **AV corridor ownership**: Waabi completed its first commercial week and Kodiak is now on Dallas-Laredo — one of UF's most strategic US-Mexico corridors. What is the explicit route exclusivity or priority lane structure in the Waabi partnership, and does UF have a mechanism to add routes proactively before Aurora and Kodiak lock in the cross-border and Midwest lanes that anchor UF's volume?

2. **Fuel surcharge recalibration under the crude/diesel split**: Crude has normalized through Hormuz but refined product flows remain at 19% of pre-war baseline, meaning diesel is structurally elevated independent of geopolitical resolution. Does Transplace's FSC automation for managed transportation accounts accurately reflect the crude/diesel divergence — and are UF's carrier partners being fairly compensated under current contract terms heading into Q4 renewals?

3. **Q4 TMS RFP readiness**: The 2027 3PL Study confirms shippers are using AI at 66% vs. 3PLs at 43% — the largest adoption gap yet measured — and Trimble's ownership is uncertain. What is Transplace's quantified AI value proposition for Q4 enterprise RFPs, and is the Erin Mitchell SVP Implementation hire adequately resourced to convert the 1-2 largest displacement opportunities this cycle?
