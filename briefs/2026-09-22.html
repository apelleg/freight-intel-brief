🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-22

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

Uber Freight announced a major European 4PL expansion today: Mike Doucleff named Head of Europe (reporting directly to CEO Rebecca Tinucci), a new Control Tower and operations hub launching in Krakow, Poland in 2027, and dedicated product and engineering resources committed to improving the European TMS experience. Coming one week after the OXEA global managed transportation mandate, this is Uber Freight's clearest statement yet that 4PL is a global, not merely North American, growth vector — and that European TMS investment is now explicitly on the product roadmap. For a CPO one hundred days into the role, this defines the geographic scope of the platform buildout: European customs workflows, shipper interfaces, and lane economics all need representation on the near-term roadmap. (Sept 22 — GlobeNewsWire)
