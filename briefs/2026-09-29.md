### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **Canada import ban in force (Sept 29):** The outright prohibition on Canadian dairy (whey, molasses), alcoholic beverages, and large-displacement motorcycles took effect at midnight today — escalating from the Section 338 50% tariff effective September 8. Goods not entered for consumption before midnight retain the 50% tariff rate; new shipments in these categories face a full ban. Separate 50% tariffs on Canadian automotive, parts, and steel are scheduled for January 1, 2027. 4PL managed transportation clients with cross-border reefer flows should receive immediate lane impact assessments. ([Sept 29, 2026 — NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/motorcycles-booze-us-ban-1-billion-canadian-imports-takes-effect-rcna600421))
