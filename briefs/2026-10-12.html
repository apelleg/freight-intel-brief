🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-12

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

The AV freight market is now operational across four simultaneous commercial deployments: Waabi/Warp completed its first commercial week on Dallas-Houston (launched Oct 5), Kodiak AI launched a new 435-mile Dallas-Laredo lane with Charger USA (Oct 8), Einride began daily driverless electric freight in Ohio (Oct 6), and Aurora remains on track for 200 driverless trucks by year-end. For Uber Freight, the Waabi TaaS partnership is now generating real freight revenue — validating the commercial AV brokerage thesis — but Kodiak simultaneously opened the Dallas-Laredo corridor that sits at the heart of UF's US-Mexico cross-border volume. The window to define which corridors the Waabi partnership owns, before competitors lock them in, is open right now.

📊 Market Pulse (30 seconds)

  • Spot rates: DAT van all-in ~$3.08/mi nationally (Oct 2026 — DAT Trendlines); linehaul $2.25/mi for week Sept 27–Oct 3 (+43% YoY, +7¢ WoW); SONAR contract TL $2.72/mi+fuel (+18% YoY, 52-week high) (Oct 9, 2026 — FreightWaves/SONAR)
  • Load-to-truck: Van L/T 13.7 (week Sept 27–Oct 3, quarter-end surge; prior weeks ~11.1–11.2); reefer 19.1; tender rejections ~14% (~3× 2023 baseline)
  • Notable capacity event: Crude oil flows through Hormuz recovered to pre-war baseline via pipeline rerouting — but refined product exports remain at only 677K bpd (19% of the 3.6M bpd pre-war level), structurally keeping diesel elevated (Oct 11, 2026 — Forbes/Kpler). EIA diesel $6.199/gal week of Oct 5 (down from $6.549 Sept 28 record); AAA spot crept back to $6.28/gal by Oct 8. Domestic intermodal at all-time volume high (+8% YoY) as diesel economics drive modal shift on lanes >500 miles (Oct 9, 2026 — FreightWaves).

🏢 Competitor Moves

  • Waabi / Warp + Uber Freight: Completed first commercial week on the Dallas-Houston LTL corridor (launched Oct 5, 2026 — Waabi). Uber Freight is the carrier network partner and commercial route developer for Warp. Implication for UF: Week-one operational data (load counts, on-time performance, customer acceptance) is now available internally — this is the foundation of the Q4 and 2027 scale-up business case. Confidence: High
  • Kodiak AI + Charger USA: Launched 435-mile Dallas-Laredo autonomous freight lane (Oct 8, 2026 — FreightWaves). Safety driver still in cab; Kodiak safety case at 96% completion; driverless launch targeted later in 2026; first AV freight lane serving Laredo, the #1 US-Mexico land port by volume. Implication for UF: Kodiak now operates on two commercial AV corridors (Dallas-Houston via IKEA, Dallas-Laredo via Charger) that directly overlap UF's highest-volume US-Mexico and Texas lanes — the brokerage race for AV corridor lock-in is underway. Confidence: High
  • Einride + EASE Logistics: Launched daily driverless Level-4 electric freight between warehouses in Marysville, Ohio (Oct 6-7, 2026 — FreightWaves/CCJ Digital). Cabless trucks, remote operator monitoring, no safety driver onboard; part of $8.8M ODOT/DriveOhio initiative. Implication for UF: AV freight is no longer confined to Texas sun-belt corridors — the Midwest industrial belt is in play, expanding the geographic TaaS addressable market and raising shipper expectations for AV availability. Confidence: High

🔧 Freight Tech & AI

  • Freight MCP ecosystem achieves critical mass: Two major freight intermediaries — Flexport (Oct 6) and Freight Right Global Logistics (Oct 9) — launched MCP APIs within 5 days of each other, enabling AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot) to book and track ocean and cross-border freight programmatically at negotiated rates, without human login (Oct 9, 2026 — FreightWaves). The MCP pattern is standardizing AI-to-freight-execution integration faster than expected; UF's own API/integration surface for managed transportation customers will be evaluated against this standard in 2027 RFPs.
  • 2027 3PL Study confirms shippers are outpacing their 3PLs on AI adoption: Shippers report 66% AI adoption (predictive analytics, risk planning) vs. only 43% for 3PLs — the widest gap yet measured (Oct 7-8, 2026 — TheTrucker/SDCExec). Top 3PL barriers: data quality and availability (61%), legacy system integration (54%), budget (46%). For UF's Transplace-based managed transportation business, this gap is the core product/engineering challenge for 2027.

🗂️ TMS & Managed Transportation

  • Trimble TMS sale still in limbo through Q4 RFP season: CEO confirmed multiple external parties remain interested in the Transportation division (~Oct 1, 2026 — AMB Logistic). Apax and Bain have stepped back; a third buyer is evaluating whether to pursue Transporeon (European) and TMW/TM4 (North American) separately. With Trimble ownership unresolved and Q4 enterprise TMS RFPs now live, enterprise shippers are evaluating alternatives with no Trimble roadmap certainty — the clearest Transplace displacement window in years.

📋 Regulatory Watch

  • FMCSA ELP NPRM comment period closed Oct 9: Over 1,800 comments received on docket FMCSA-2026-0826; the proposed rule would codify English language proficiency failures as out-of-service violations, potentially sidelining ~9,000 additional cross-border drivers on top of 26,000+ already out of market (Oct 9, 2026 — Federal Register/FreightWaves). FMCSA is now reviewing comments with no published timeline for a final rule. Combined with diesel economics, this represents the second cross-border supply-side constraint at active regulatory risk entering Q4.
