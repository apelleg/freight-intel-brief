🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-17

⚡ Today's Top Signal

Dry van spot rates posted their largest single-week drop since 2008 — falling $0.06/mile to $2.28 for the week of Aug 10–16 — yet remain 42% above year-ago levels, marking the arrival of post-July pull-forward normalization. The window for rate relief is likely brief: Canada Section 338 tariffs (50% duties on $20B of Canadian goods including autos, lumber, and dairy) take effect Tuesday Aug 19 with no USMCA exemption, and CVSA Brake Safety Week follows Aug 23–29 with a historical 14.3% truck OOS rate. For Uber Freight's brokerage, a contract conversion window opens today and closes by end of week.

Shippers can no longer count on contract as a hedge — parity is the new baseline. This is the clearest product opening in two years to pitch dynamic pricing or hybrid contract structures. (Aug 11, 2026 — DAT / FreightWaves)

No supply disruption yet, but carrier cost modeling should flag this risk. (Aug 15, 2026 — Fortune / Bloomberg)
