## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-07
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Iran and Oman reached a framework agreement on August 5–6 establishing new shipping routes through the Strait of Hormuz — the first concrete step toward reopening the waterway after 165+ days of effective closure. Oil fell to $75–82/bbl on the news; national diesel sits at $5.13/gal. For Uber Freight, this is a structural cost-of-service signal: sustained diesel relief compresses the fuel surcharge embedded in elevated spot rates (+42% YoY), which benefits shipper cost positions and could loosen the rate environment heading into Q3. Critically, the deal is not yet implemented — Iran's draft conditions restrict U.S. and Israeli vessel access — so freight cost relief is directional rather than certain, and carriers remain reluctant to discount until diesel actually moves.

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Canada tariff front-loading now:** Section 338 tariffs take effect August 19 — 12 days away — and Canada-US lanes are experiencing peak booking urgency this week. Does the Transplace TMS surface real-time Canada-US lane capacity constraints to managed transportation customers, and are we proactively pushing automated rerouting and mode-switch recommendations to shippers with Canadian supply exposure before slots fill?

2. **Profitability gap vs. RXO:** RXO posted $40M Q2 EBITDA; Uber Freight ran at −$24M operating loss — both growing at 25% revenue. If managed transportation is the profitability lever, what is Transplace's specific EBITDA contribution today, and what does our Q3 managed transportation pipeline look like against RXO's disclosed $200M+?

3. **FMCSA ADS NPRM public comment:** The FMCSA ADS rule (RIN 2126-AC17) is expected to publish this month and will shape driver qualification equivalencies and inspection standards for driverless trucks. As Aurora's named freight network partner, Uber Freight has standing to shape how these standards are written. Do we have a policy position ready, and are we coordinating with Aurora on a joint comment before the window opens?
