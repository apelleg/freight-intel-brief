## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-30
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

---

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

The Strait of Hormuz is quietly reopening: commercial oil volumes reached 13.1 million barrels/day last week — 77% of the pre-war baseline — as Iran's blocking leverage erodes under U.S. military escort operations and a "dark tanker shuttle" system (AIS off, ship-to-ship transfers in the Gulf of Oman). The EIA's official diesel survey printed $6.382/gal on September 29, down 17 cents from the $6.549 all-time record — the first confirmed pullback. If Hormuz normalization holds into Q4, the diesel price shock that has been Uber Freight's primary rate tailwind begins unwinding — exactly as carriers push to lock 2027 contracts at inflated levels. This is the most consequential macro inflection for UF's commercial team in months.

*(September 29, 2026 — [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/sep/29/hormuz-traffic-nears-prewar-levels-iran-war-simmers/) / [The National News](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/09/29/oil-flows-through-strait-of-hormuz-rise-on-saudi-pivot-as-red-sea-traffic-plunges/) / [EIA](https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/gasdiesel/))*

(2) Panama Canal lowers max vessel draft to 14.48m effective October 1 (from 14.63m), further constraining transpacific import capacity heading into peak. *(September 29, 2026 — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/29/canada-import-ban-trade-war.html) / [AlJazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/8/panama-canal-may-cut-ship-traffic-further-as-el-nio-strengthens))*

CEO Rob Painter said "no update" on the Goldman Sachs–led sale process; he's "super comfortable" owning the business. *Implication for UF: Trimble's product push stabilizes its Q4 RFP narrative while the sale overhang persists — Transplace's displacement window stays open through year-end. New: Aurora Driver 2 commercial platform, next-gen hardware (AUMOVIO partnership), PPAP by end-2027. *Implication for UF: UF's Waabi partnership routes around Aurora's direct carrier deployments (FedEx Freight, Werner); Aurora's 200-truck year-end target means Waabi's first-mover advantage in UF's lane network holds for now.* Confidence: High *(September 29, 2026 — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/aurora-at-evercore-forum-driverless-trucking-moves-toward-scale-93CH-4922914))*

### 📋 Regulatory Watch

- **Panama Canal draft cut** (effective October 1): Max draft drops to 14.48m (from 14.63m), with 32 daily transits capped (9 Neopanamax, 23 Panamax). El Niño watershed 35.8% below historical average; further cuts possible in Q1 2027 depending on Oct–Nov rainfall. Impact: additional transpacific volume compression forces import overflow toward domestic intermodal and truck as peak season approaches. *(September 2026 — [AlJazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/8/panama-canal-may-cut-ship-traffic-further-as-el-nio-strengthens) / [MarineInsight](https://www.marineinsight.com/panama-canal-to-cut-ship-transits-again-due-to-severe-drought-linked-to-el-nino/))*

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Hormuz Normalization & Rate Floor Risk:** Diesel printed its first meaningful decline ($6.382/gal, -17 cents from record) as Hormuz oil volumes recover to 77% of pre-war baseline. If fuel normalizes into Q4, the diesel-driven rate premium may reverse — exactly as we're locking 2027 contract rates with carriers and shippers. What's our freight rate assumption for diesel in 2027 bids, and do our contracts have fuel-step clauses that protect us if diesel falls 15–20% from current levels?

2. **Transplace Visibility at Trimble's Moment:** Trimble just ran a major product conference that directly addressed Q4 shipper RFP concerns — agent-ready TMS, 20 new features, CEO reassurance on continuity. Our Transplace TMS competes in the same deals. Do we have a comparable product launch, customer event, or analyst visibility moment planned before year-end — and does the Transplace roadmap read as clearly to shippers who are evaluating now?

3. **AV Partner Stack: Should We Add Kodiak?** Kodiak is 93% toward its first driverless commercial run on Texas and California lanes — the same geography as our Waabi partnership. With two first-wave AV players approaching launch on overlapping corridors, should UF pursue a Kodiak commercial arrangement now (before C.H. Robinson or Echo does) to give managed transportation clients a multi-AV-vendor option on their routing guides?
