## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-24
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Last-ditch US-Canada negotiations collapsed Friday night, and Section 338 tariffs — 50% duties on ~$20B of Canadian goods including autos, dairy, alcohol, lumber, cement, machinery, and electronics — took effect 12:01 AM ET August 22 with **no USMCA exemption**. PM Carney recalled Canada's negotiating team, declared Canada "at war economically" with the US, and announced dollar-for-dollar retaliation starting September 8 targeting US steel, dairy, appliances, agricultural equipment, pulp, paper, and electronics. For Uber Freight, both the cross-border brokerage and Transplace 4PL lines are directly in scope: 25–30% of US-Canada cross-border trucking runs daily, tariffed goods face immediate cost shock, and September 8 creates mirror-image disruption for US shippers exporting to Canada. The window to help enterprise accounts remodel cross-border supply chains — and win managed transportation business — is open now.

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Canada 338 as a 4PL Sales Moment:** With 50% duties live on both sides of the border by Sept 8, enterprise shippers in autos, food & beverage, and industrials face urgent pressure to remodel sourcing, inventory buffers, and mode selection. Does Uber Freight's Transplace platform have live tariff impact modeling? Can we deliver a Canada 338 disruption analysis to our top 20 managed accounts within a week? This kind of consultative crisis response is what converts brokerage relationships into long-term 4PL contracts.

2. **Fuel Surcharge Calibration for a $5.75+ Diesel World:** Bessent's secondary sanctions could squeeze Hormuz transits further, and diesel has already surged to $5.45/gal heading into September. Do our managed transportation contracts and brokerage FSC schedules adequately protect UF margin and carrier relationships at $5.75–6.00 diesel? The Transplace-managed side carries more direct cost exposure than brokerage; worth stress-testing before September contracts reprice.

3. **FMCSA ADS NPRM: Should Uber Freight File a Comment?** NHTSA's comment period closes August 31 (one week), and FMCSA's own ADS NPRM for driverless commercial trucks drops any day. As Aurora's named commercial network partner under the DaaS model, Uber Freight has a direct commercial stake in how CDL equivalency, pre-trip inspection, and liability standards are written for autonomous loads. What position should UF take on broker liability in DaaS-assigned loads?
