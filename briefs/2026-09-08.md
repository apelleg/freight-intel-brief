## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-08
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Canada's retaliatory counter-tariffs — 15–50% on C$27.6B of US exports across 700+ product lines (steel, dairy, appliances, electronics, agricultural equipment) — took effect at midnight, completing the two-wall tariff structure on US-Canada trade. US Section 338 (50% on $20B of Canadian goods) has been live since August 22; Canada's retaliation is now live today. USTR Greer has confirmed **no further US-Canada trade talks are planned** — this is a settled bifurcation, not a negotiating posture. For Uber Freight: northbound US-Canada freight demand is in immediate disruption as carriers actively pull equipment from those lanes. The tariff shock structurally accelerates shipper migration toward US-Mexico corridors — the exact trade lane where Uber Freight has been investing. Q4 peak season begins with this asymmetry fully in place.

### 📊 Market Pulse (30 seconds)
- **Spot rates:** Dry van linehaul $2.19/mi (week ending Sept 1, −$0.02 WoW, +38% YoY); reefer spot posted its sharpest weekly gain of the post-Labor Day period this week — seasonal reversal is underway (September 7–8, 2026 — DAT / FleetOwner)
- **Load-to-truck:** 12.01 at month-end (up from 9.62 one week prior); load posts +12.3% WoW, truck posts −10.0% WoW — tightening expected to accelerate through peak season
- **Notable capacity event:** Triple-shock environment entering peak — (1) Canada counter-tariffs live today; (2) Panama Canal drops to 32 daily transits September 15 (from 34, El Niño watershed 44% below historical average), forcing East Coast and Gulf port rerouting; (3) Hormuz Day 191: ~12 ships/day September 6 — slight improvement from the ~6/day nadir on September 1, still vs. 85/day normal; diesel $5.599/gal as of August 31, rising. Separately, USTR is now signaling "interim arrangements" rather than a full USMCA deal for 2026, with structural rework likely slipping to 2027 — US-Mexico cross-border uncertainty persists through next contract year
