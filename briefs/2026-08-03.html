  • Canada Section 338 tariffs — T-16 days (effective August 19): 50% additional duties on Canadian imports take effect August 19 with no USMCA carve-out. Covered goods include wood, paper, dairy, alcohol, machinery, furniture, and textiles — affecting an estimated $20B in annual Canadian imports.
