## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-02
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
September opens with three supply-side shocks converging inside six days: Panama Canal transit slots drop to 34/day on September 4 (then 32/day September 15); Canada's retaliatory counter-tariffs (15–50% on $27.6B in U.S. goods, 700+ product lines) land September 8 with USTR Greer confirming no further US-Canada talks; and the Strait of Hormuz remains near-closed at 3–15 vessel transits per day vs. ~130 pre-war. DAT's August monthly report confirms van TVI at its softest since 2021, yet the month-end L/T ratio spiked to 12.01 from 9.62 in a single week — capacity attrition, not demand, is the active driver. For Uber Freight, the next 6 days require simultaneous triage on cross-border Canadian lane exposure (4PL managed transportation clients) and a tightening spot market that may reverse the summer softness faster than the demand-side would suggest.
