### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Saudi Arabia's East-West crude pipeline (4–5M bpd) was shut September 10–11 after Iraqi drone strikes, compounding the ongoing Hormuz crisis to push Brent oil to **$110.42/bbl** and U.S. diesel to an **all-time record $6.23/gallon** (AAA, Sept 14). Today in Muscat, Iran and Oman are formally signing a designated shipping route through Hormuz — a partial relief valve, but Iran has stated it does not consider the strait "open." For Uber Freight, two immediate questions: (1) Are every Transplace-based managed transportation client's fuel surcharge clauses correctly passing through at $6.23/gallon — at ~28% of carrier cost-per-mile, any FSC gap is now a P&L emergency; (2) if the Muscat route moves tankers and Brent corrects sharply, the market will flip from carrier-favorable to shipper-favorable within days, and UF must be positioned to act on both sides of that trade.
