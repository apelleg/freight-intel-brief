## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-27
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Oil posted a fourth consecutive session of declines toward $80–81/barrel as Iran and Oman reached a maritime water-sharing agreement on the Strait of Hormuz, and President Trump reported 10 million barrels transited on Tuesday — the clearest signal yet that 6 months of Hormuz disruption may be easing. For Uber Freight, this is the critical inflection: diesel at a 2026 high of $5.45/gallon has been a structural prop under elevated truckload rates; if crude retreats meaningfully through September, fuel surcharge revenues compress and carrier cost expectations reset — precisely as Q4 contract negotiations begin. The window to model post-Hormuz contract repricing scenarios for key shipper accounts is now, not after the straits formally reopen.

1. **Hormuz de-escalation and contract repricing:** If diesel retreats from $5.45/gal toward $4.50 by October as Hormuz access improves, what happens to Uber Freight's fuel surcharge revenue model and Q4 contract rate guidance — and are we proactively modeling those scenarios for key shipper accounts before they do?
