**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Two of freight's biggest AI-tech data points drop simultaneously today: C.H. Robinson reports Q2 earnings after the bell (consensus $1.51 EPS, +17% YoY), and Aurora Innovation holds its Q2 Business Review — the first formal commercial read since Gen-2 driverless trucks commercially launched July 22. For Uber Freight as both a competing broker and Aurora's named commercial freight customer, both calls will reshape competitive positioning: CHR's call will prove or disprove whether AI-driven brokerage monetizes at scale, and Aurora's call sets the baseline for how much autonomous truck capacity flows through Uber Freight's network through year-end. Three rules already effective July 22 (CDL self-reporting eliminated, physical ELD manual removed, inspection report scope narrowed). Further deregulatory actions could meaningfully reduce carrier compliance costs — partially offsetting financial pressure from elevated spot rates and post-Montgomery insurance premium increases on managed transportation carrier networks. ([July 29, 2026 — Land Line Media](https://landline.media/truckers-have-through-tuesday-july-29-to-comment-on-deregulatory-actions/))

### 📋 Regulatory Watch
- **Canada Section 338 tariffs: T-21 days (August 19):** 50% additional duties on ~$20B of Canadian imports — autos, dairy, alcohol, wood products, paper, machinery, textiles — with USMCA-qualifying goods explicitly NOT exempt. This is a major break from prior Canada tariff actions: USMCA compliance provides zero protection. FTZ filings must be in privileged-foreign-status before August 19 or goods inherit the duty. Stacks on Section 301 (live since July 24) to create the most compressed dual-tariff shock since IEEPA spring 2026. Cross-border managed transportation clients need lane-by-lane P&L repricing now. ([July 21, 2026 — Presidential Proclamation; Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/unitedstates/export-controls-trade-investment-sanctions/1823950/new-section-338-actions-against-canada-and-section-301-tariffs-on-60-trading-partners-and-brazil-key-considerations-for-us-importers); [Crane Worldwide](https://www.craneww.com/knowledge-center/trade-advisory-notices/trade-advisory-new-section-338-tariffs-add-50-duties-on-certain-canadian-imports-effective-august-19-2026/))

- **USMCA Round 3 concluded (July 21-23, Mexico City):** US and Mexico reported progress on automotive rules of origin (US demands 82% RVC + 50% US-specific content + Chinese component restrictions), agriculture, steel, and labor. Canada excluded from talks while simultaneously hit with 50% Section 338 tariffs. July 1 joint review deadline already missed; no full renewal expected near-term; annual review cycle now running through at least Q1 2027. ([July 24, 2026 — USTR](https://ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/july/united-states-and-mexico-convene-mexico-city-third-bilateral-negotiating-round-related-joint-review); [AS/COA](https://www.as-coa.org/articles/tracking-us-mexico-talks-usmca-review))

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Aurora allocation strategy for Gen-2 fleet:** Today's Q2 Business Review will give the first hard commercial metrics on Aurora's 200-truck year-end trajectory. With Roush targeting 1,000 trucks/year production and customer sales beginning in 2027, the window for preferential allocation terms is now — before other carrier-partners lock in commitments. Should we be pursuing a preferred commercial allocation agreement that reserves a larger share of 2027 Aurora capacity for Uber Freight's network, and does our BD team have a proposal ready?

2. **Trimble TMS client outreach as managed-transportation sales window:** Mid-market shippers running TMW are in platform uncertainty as Trimble's sale process unfolds. This is a 60-90 day window — the kind that closes before you notice it. Does our managed transportation sales team have a targeted list of Trimble TMW clients, and is our Transplace-based offering positioned with a clear migration narrative that speaks to these shippers' specific integration needs?

3. **Canada Section 338 client preparedness (T-21 days):** August 19 is close and USMCA provides zero protection against the 50% tariff on covered Canadian goods. For 4PL and managed transportation clients with Canadian import exposure, the Transplace platform should be running scenario analysis this week — lane re-optimization, bonded warehouse modeling, mode shifts to intermodal where possible. Which accounts need proactive outreach from our team, and can we turn this into a value-demonstrating engagement before competitors do?
