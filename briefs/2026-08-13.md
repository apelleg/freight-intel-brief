## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-13
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Uber Freight's own data infrastructure is today's lead: the Helix extortion group (identified by Google Mandiant as UNC6671) claims to have exfiltrated approximately 1 million files from Uber Freight systems — dispatch documents, accounts payable/receivable data, mailbox archives, and OneDrive files. Uber Freight confirmed the incident was "identified, contained, and remediated" with federal law enforcement engaged, but has not disclosed whether shipper contract pricing or carrier rate data are among the stolen materials. The competitive and legal exposure is immediate: if routing logic, spot pricing, or managed-transportation contract terms are in the wild, the CPO must lead both a client notification decision and an internal data architecture review — and the window for getting ahead of the story is closing. (August 12, 2026 — TechCrunch, The Register, SC Media)
