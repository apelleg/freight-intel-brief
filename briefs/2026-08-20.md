Hours after 50% Section 338 tariffs on ~$20B in Canadian goods went live at 12:01am ET August 19, the White House paused them through end-of-day Friday, August 21, with President Trump describing the emerging framework as "very good for both countries." The 48-hour countdown is live: if a deal closes by Friday EOD, pent-up freight staged in Canada will release in a rush; if talks stall, the 50% duty triggers automatically on Saturday. Every carrier and shipper moving Canada-origin goods faces a real-time hold-or-release call — and Uber Freight's cross-border brokerage and Transplace-managed shipper networks need surge protocols ready now.

- **Canada Section 338 tariffs: PAUSED, deal pending by Aug 21 EOD.** Tariffs went live midnight Aug 19, paused within hours for 3 days. Proposed deal terms: US lowers tariffs on Canadian autos, steel, aluminum, and forest products; Canada concedes on alcohol, dairy, and procurement. Does UF's cross-border brokerage have pre-positioned capacity, and can Transplace-managed shipper workflows handle a sudden Canada-origin volume spike without manual firefighting?
