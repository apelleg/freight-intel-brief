## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-17
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Dry van spot rates posted their largest single-week drop since 2008 — falling $0.06/mile to $2.28 for the week of Aug 10–16 — yet remain 42% above year-ago levels, marking the arrival of post-July pull-forward normalization. The window for rate relief is likely brief: Canada Section 338 tariffs (50% duties on $20B of Canadian goods including autos, lumber, and dairy) take effect Tuesday Aug 19 with no USMCA exemption, and CVSA Brake Safety Week follows Aug 23–29 with a historical 14.3% truck OOS rate. For Uber Freight's brokerage, a contract conversion window opens today and closes by end of week.

### 🔧 Freight Tech & AI

- **Spot-contract rate parity: a dynamic pricing signal.** DAT data shows spot dry van converged with contract rates for the first time since February 2022, after contracts adjusted up $0.13/mile (van) and $0.09/mile (reefer) over the past month. Shippers can no longer count on contract as a hedge — parity is the new baseline. This is the clearest product opening in two years to pitch dynamic pricing or hybrid contract structures. ([Aug 11, 2026 — DAT / FreightWaves](https://www.dat.com/))
- **Iran-Oman Hormuz talks stall — diesel cost watch.** Iran and Oman are finalizing a navigation route map in Strait of Hormuz security talks, but attacks continue mounting (65 incidents, 17 seafarers dead; ADNOC vessels recently hit). Brent crude rose ~6% this week on fading quick-resolution hopes. Sustained crude elevation translates to diesel pressure on carrier operating costs within 4–6 weeks, historically passed through as fuel surcharge adjustments. No supply disruption yet, but carrier cost modeling should flag this risk. ([Aug 15, 2026 — Fortune / Bloomberg](https://fortune.com/))
