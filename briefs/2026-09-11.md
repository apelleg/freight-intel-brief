## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-11
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
Iran launched its largest-ever wave of attacks on Strait of Hormuz shipping on September 9, pushing Brent crude to $97–$99/barrel — Goldman Sachs now warns $120/barrel is plausible in 2027 if 4 million bpd of Gulf output remains offline. Diesel hit an all-time high of nearly $6/gallon in the first week of September, colliding with the tightest truckload market since 2021 (NTI $3.44/mile, +47% YoY; van load-to-truck 11.5; reefer tender rejections above 20%). For Uber Freight, this double shock — record fuel costs crushing carrier margins and record rejection rates squeezing shippers — arrives exactly as Q4 managed transportation budgets are being finalized under pre-negotiated Transplace contracts.

Shippers restructuring cross-border programs mid-cycle need managed transportation partners equipped for tariff-driven lane volatility — a core Uber Freight/Transplace 4PL value proposition. (September 2026 — C.H. Robinson / GHY)
