💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

  1. CHR's RICO Defection Window: CHR is managing a federal RICO lawsuit alleging systematic routing to chameleon carriers — while simultaneously running Q4 contract renewals with shippers at +30% YoY pricing. Shipper frustration with both CHR's liability exposure and rate increases creates a genuine defection window in Q4. Where should UF's product and GTM teams focus to capture CHR accounts in motion — and do we have the onboarding tooling to absorb volume quickly?
  2. Mexico Structural Capacity Resilience in Transplace: The CANACAR strike ended in 2 days, but 25,000+ Mexican commercial drivers remain sidelined by B-1 visa revocations with no near-term political resolution. Should the Transplace TMS product team build a Mexico capacity resilience module — real-time eligible-carrier tracking, automated alternative routing, and proactive shipper alerts — that can activate when cross-border events disrupt capacity, rather than relying on manual response?
  3. AV Lanes as a Product Differentiator Before Commoditization: Aurora has named Uber Freight as a commercial customer, and its DaaS network is now scaling toward 200+ driverless trucks by Dec 31 with multiple committed partners. The window where UF can offer AV capacity as a differentiated product experience — before it's available at parity to all brokers — is narrowing. What does the shipper-facing UX for AV lane availability, pricing transparency, and SLA commitments look like today, and what would it take to make it a reason shippers choose UF over CHR or Flexport for high-value lanes?
