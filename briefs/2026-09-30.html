🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-30

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

The Strait of Hormuz is quietly reopening: commercial oil volumes reached 13.1 million barrels/day last week — 77% of the pre-war baseline — as Iran's blocking leverage erodes under U.S. military escort operations and a "dark tanker shuttle" system (AIS off, ship-to-ship transfers in the Gulf of Oman). The EIA's official diesel survey printed $6.382/gal on September 29, down 17 cents from the $6.549 all-time record — the first confirmed pullback. If Hormuz normalization holds into Q4, the diesel price shock that has been Uber Freight's primary rate tailwind begins unwinding — exactly as carriers push to lock 2027 contracts at inflated levels. This is the most consequential macro inflection for UF's commercial team in months.

(September 29, 2026 — Washington Times / The National News / EIA)

CEO Rob Painter said "no update" on the Goldman Sachs–led sale process; he's "super comfortable" owning the business. Implication for UF: Trimble's product push stabilizes its Q4 RFP narrative while the sale overhang persists — Transplace's displacement window stays open through year-end. Counter-move: Transplace needs a comparable product visibility moment before the RFP window closes. Confidence: High (September 28, 2026 — FreightWaves)

Implication for UF: UF's Waabi partnership routes around Aurora's direct carrier deployments (FedEx Freight, Werner); Aurora's 200-truck year-end target means Waabi's first-mover advantage in UF's lane network holds for now. Confidence: High (September 29, 2026 — Investing.com)

Kodiak operates on Texas/Permian Basin and California (Fresno→LA) lanes. Implication for UF: A second AV carrier is approaching launch on the same Texas corridor as Waabi — UF should evaluate whether to add Kodiak to its AV partner stack before a competitor does. Confidence: High (September 29, 2026 — Investing.com)
