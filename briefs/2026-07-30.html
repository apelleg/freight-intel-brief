⚡ Today's Top Signal

The Q2 earnings trifecta delivered the market's clearest read yet on who's winning the freight AI race. C.H. Robinson reported $4.93B in revenue (+19% YoY) and 60% cumulative AI productivity gains since 2022 — with 30+ autonomous agents in live brokerage — while XPO posted its best-ever North American LTL adjusted operating ratio of 79.9% (a 300bps record) in the same morning session. Aurora confirmed 440,000 driverless miles and a new temperature-controlled freight deal. For Uber Freight, these three results read together as a single verdict: the operators who invested in technology during the freight recession are now extracting outsized margins in the upturn, and the gap is compounding. The question isn't whether AI-driven efficiency creates competitive moats in freight brokerage — CHR just proved it does — it's whether Uber Freight's own roadmap can match that pace.
