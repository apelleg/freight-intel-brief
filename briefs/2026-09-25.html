🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-09-25

Read time: ~4 minutes

⚡ Today's Top Signal

Super Typhoon Ragasa made landfall on the South China coast on September 24, disrupting Shanghai and Ningbo port operations with 661 vessels already impacted or at risk, pushing Shanghai–LA transpacific spot rates to $7,185/FEU. The compounding factor for domestic freight: JB Hunt this week announced its intermodal capacity has hit its ceiling, forcing overflow freight onto spot truck — at a moment when tender rejections are back above 14% and diesel sits at a record $6.53/gallon. For Uber Freight, this is a brokerage revenue moment: dray and spot truck demand on the West Coast is about to spike, but 4PL clients simultaneously face mounting fuel surcharges and expedite costs that Uber Freight's managed transportation team will need to absorb or renegotiate.

Confidence: High (Sept 25, 2026 — Supply Chain Dive, CCJ)
  • Kodiak AI / DTL Transport: Completed first autonomous produce deliveries in California under a new CA DMV heavy-duty AV Testing permit — 225-mile Fresno→LA runs on SR-99 and I-5 with a safety driver present; plans driverless Texas operations by year-end. With Roush Industries targeting 1,000-unit annual production of Kodiak Driver-equipped trucks, Kodiak is now the second AV player with permitted freight routes in two major states (Texas + California). Implication for UF: Uber Freight's Waabi partnership now faces a competitor directly in the I-5/SR-99 reefer corridor — California's highest-volume produce lane. Confidence: High (Sept 24, 2026 — GlobeNewswire, Heavy Duty Trucking)

Implication for UF: Accelerating carrier exit keeps supply tight and spot rates elevated; it also shrinks the approved-carrier pool, reinforcing Uber Freight's carrier vetting infrastructure as a moat against brokers with weaker compliance capabilities. Confidence: High (Sept 21, 2026 — FreightWaves)

🔧 Freight Tech & AI

  • AV competitive map: Kodiak in California (Sept 24 — see Competitor Moves). With Aurora tracking toward 200 driverless trucks by year-end and Volvo launching 300+ Aurora-powered VNLs in Q1 2027, the deployment race has shifted from "who's first?" to "who's scaling fastest in which corridors?" The California reefer lane now has a permitted AV pilot competitor. (CNBC Aurora, GlobeNewswire)

The differentiation window is narrowing: the moat is shifting from "having AI" to owning proprietary data at scale and the logistics graph behind it. (Sept 25, 2026 — Heavy Duty Trucking)
