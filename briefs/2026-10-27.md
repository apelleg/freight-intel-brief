## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-10-27
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Mexico's CANACAR launched an indefinite nationwide trucking stoppage Monday, October 26 — the same week US-Mexico bilateral USMCA talks stalled ahead of a soft November deadline. The strike is active across 19 states, with organizers estimating up to 21,000 daily border crossings worth ~$1.8B at risk. Stacked on top of the B-1 visa crackdown that already sidelined 25,000+ Mexican commercial drivers (ongoing since October), cross-border TL capacity is entering its tightest conditions of the 2026 cycle — exactly as Q4 peak demand builds. Uber Freight's cross-border brokerage and managed transportation customers with Mexico-origin freight face direct, acute exposure.

### 📊 Market Pulse (30 seconds)
- **Spot rates:** Van all-in $3.07/mi (down 2¢ WoW); linehaul $2.24/mi (+33% YoY). Cooling modestly from June 2026 record of $3.83/mi but capacity constraints are holding the floor. *(Oct 27, 2026 — DAT Freight & Analytics)*
- **Load-to-truck:** Van 9.51 (Week 43, Oct 19–25), down from 9.70 prior week. Reefer 14.8 (down from 15.8). Flatbed 34.9 (down from 36.5). Directional easing, but van L/T remains 57% above year-ago level of 6.03. *(Oct 27, 2026 — DAT)*
- **Notable capacity event:** CANACAR indefinite freight stoppage active since Oct 26 across 19 Mexican states; threatens Laredo, Otay Mesa, and Calexico cross-border corridors. Combined with ongoing B-1 visa crackdown, cross-border TL in acute stress heading into Q4 peak.

Aurora targets 200+ driverless trucks by Dec 31, and Uber Freight is a named customer. *Implication for UF: 2027 AV capacity integration is no longer a planning horizon — it's an execution timeline. Confidence: High* *(Oct 26, 2026 — FreightWaves/BusinessWire)*
