### ⚡ Today's Top Signal

Canada's bilateral tariff wall is now complete: U.S. Section 338 tariffs (50% on Canadian goods, live Aug 22) are matched by Canada's counter-tariffs (15–50% on C$27.6B in U.S. goods, effective Sept 8). USTR Greer confirmed no dialogue with Ottawa. Simultaneously, the post-Labor Day freight reversal is accelerating — tender rejection rates surged to 14.5% over Labor Day weekend, the highest since the 2021 cycle peak, settling ~14% as of Sept 8. For Uber Freight, these two shocks converge into Q4 with precise timing: tariff-driven lane disruption forces shippers to restructure cross-border supply chains (advisory and repricing opportunity for Transplace 4PL), while tightening capacity means brokerage spot margins expand — but 4PL clients booked at 2025 contract rates will demand renegotiation, and the brokerage with carrier depth on high-rejection lanes captures the most dislocation value.
