## 🚛 Uber Freight Intel Brief — 2026-08-12
**Read time: ~4 minutes**

### ⚡ Today's Top Signal
DAT's August monthly report (released August 11) confirmed the largest June-to-July contract rate gains on record — dry van contracts jumped 13¢/mile to $2.39/mile, with van spot rates reaching contract parity for the first time since February 2022. The signal is structurally significant: rates are rising even as volumes decline (-6% MoM for van, -5% for reefer), confirming the market is pricing carrier exit dynamics rather than demand strength. For Uber Freight, this inflection has a direct 4PL implication — Transplace customers operating under fixed-price managed transportation programs will face repricing pressure as the spot-contract gap closes, while the brokerage side has a margin-capture opportunity as spot demand intensifies.

Mid-market shippers currently on TMW are evaluating renewals under platform uncertainty — a displacement window for Transplace managed transportation proposals. (Aug 2026 — [Axios/CCJ](https://www.ccjdigital.com/business/mergers-acquisitions/article/15829584/trimble-may-sell-transportation-logistics-unit-reports-say))

Dynamic mode-mix reoptimization — intermodal on eligible lanes, LTL consolidation, cross-dock — is the differentiator enterprise shippers will demand from 4PL providers through Q3. (Aug 2026 — SONAR/DAT)

### 💡 CPO Lens: 3 Things Worth Discussing with Your Team

1. **Spot-Contract Parity and 4PL Repricing:** The moment spot crosses contract has historically triggered shippers to restructure freight programs — and it's when 4PLs either retain or lose managed transport relationships. What is UF's proactive playbook for Transplace customers whose current contract economics are now misaligned with market rates, and how do we frame repricing as a service advantage rather than a surprise cost?

2. **Canada Section 338 T-7 Days — Brokerage and 4PL Dual Exposure:** August 19 will create both a pre-deadline volume surge (this week) and a sharp volume cliff on Canada-US lanes after. How are we preparing our capacity desk for the surge and communicating the expected demand drop-off to 4PL customers who import from Canada — and are we positioned to capture brokerage share during the tightening window?

3. **FMCSA ADS NPRM Imminent:** When the first federal framework for driverless commercial trucks drops this month, Aurora — our named partner — and other ADS operators in our network will face new compliance obligations. Does UF have a carrier onboarding and compliance framework ready for ADS-equipped trucks, and how does this interact with our DocAI document handling and carrier vetting stack?
